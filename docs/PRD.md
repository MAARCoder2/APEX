# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD) — PROJECT APEX V2
**Document ID:** `PRD-APEX-V2`  
**Target Release:** M1–M5 Playable Browser Milestone  

---

## 1. Product Vision
Project Apex V2 is an authentic, browser-native 3D open-world action experience set in a Dhaka-inspired urban district (Gulshan-2). Players explore the streets on foot with smooth third-person character locomotion, enter and drive responsive high-detail vehicles with physical suspension and braking, navigate dynamic city traffic and pedestrians, and complete narrative delivery and courier missions.

## 2. Core Player Experience
1. **Third-Person Locomotion:** Natural humanoid character with skeletal Idle, Walk, and Run animation blending. Responsive WASD/keyboard movement, mouse orbit/follow camera with collision detection.
2. **Vehicle Driving & Physics:** Enter/exit vehicle interaction with proximity checks. Realistic acceleration, speed-sensitive steering, physical braking, reverse gear, and wheel rotation.
3. **Dhaka Urban Grounding:** Real geospatial road layout of Gulshan Circle 2 and Kamal Ataturk Avenue. Authentic aesthetic with Bengali storefront signage, modern high-rises, street lighting, curbs, and tropical vegetation.
4. **Traffic & Pedestrian AI:** Non-player traffic vehicles and pedestrians following waypoint navigation graphs without clipping or getting stuck.
5. **Missions & Objectives:** Structured mission loop ("The Gulshan Delivery") with checkpoint waypoints, countdown timers, minimap guidance, and rewards.
6. **Ergonomic HUD & Audio:** Zero-occlusion collapsible dock, dynamic minimap radar, speedometer, procedural Web Audio SFX (engine throttle, screech, horn, footsteps).
