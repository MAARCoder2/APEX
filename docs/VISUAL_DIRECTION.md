## 1. Aesthetic Identity: Dhaka Urbanism
- **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Golden afternoon sun with warm directional key light (`0xfff1cf`, intensity 2.2) contrasting against a cool skylight hemisphere (`0x87ceeb` sky, `0x3a4048` ground, intensity 1.1). Subtle atmospheric haze (`scene.fog = new THREE.FogExp2(0xd6e4ec, 0.0035)`) simulating Dhaka's humid tropical horizon.
- **Architectural Palette:** Concrete and plaster facades weathered with tropical stains, modern reflective glass towers with PMREM environmental reflections, commercial storefronts featuring bilingual signage (Bengali and English), rooftop water reserve tanks, and transmission antennas.
- **Streetscape:** Asphalt roads with white and yellow road divider markings, raised sidewalk curbs with hexagonal paving textures, lampposts, bus halts, and palm/neem trees lining the sidewalks.
- **Vehicles:** Gleaming metallic lacquer sports car and city sedans with realistic wheel rims, tire treads, interior dashboard, and reflective windshield glass.
